## Stage 3 — pre-registered strategy research (original 135-hypothesis intraday screen completed; daily profile now registered)

Evaluate deterministic hypotheses on sufficiently complete, timestamp-aligned multi-source history. Compare against simple trend/momentum and matched naive baselines. Use chronological train/validation/untouched holdout splits, walk-forward evaluation, multiple-testing correction, and realistic fees, spread, slippage, latency, partial fills and liquidity constraints. The new `1d` profile uses 250 train bars, 30-bar non-overlapping tests, 60 OOS windows and a global Benjamini-Hochberg correction across all four horizons. It is not eligible until each series has at least 2,563 closed daily bars; insufficient history must remain unavailable rather than reduce the sample floor. Order Block/SMC concepts remain optional unproven hypotheses; they are not a predetermined strategy or a reason to relax evidence requirements.

## Stage 4 — forward paper trading (lean MVP; experimental only, not live-authorized)

Only promote independently validated candidates. Run a continuous forward Paper ledger using Tabdeal-local prices and order-book evidence, with restart/reconciliation, stale-data, duplicate-order, daily-loss, drawdown and kill-switch tests. Report net return, drawdown, trade count, exposure, costs and comparison to suitable benchmarks. A no-trade result is valid.

## Stage 5 — capped live pilot (adapter foundation in this public PR; not deployed, enabled or authorized)
